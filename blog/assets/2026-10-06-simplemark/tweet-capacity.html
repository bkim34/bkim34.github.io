No distortion means no message. So make distortion a dial.

Bits a watermark can carry per token versus the distortion budget ε. At ε = 0 the watermarked and unwatermarked distributions coincide and nothing can be decoded. SimpleMark lets you pick ε.

Binary toy channel from the paper (Example 1): information per token = 1 − h₂(½ + ε).
